# Screenshot credits

The product tour uses Tagnetic’s dark interface captured at 2× resolution in
Linux WebKitGTK. The demo library contains 1,248 disposable FLAC files, with
archive copies of three albums. The audio is silence. The Online result cards
use fixture responses based on the releases below.

- [Discovery, Daft Punk](https://daftpunk.com/discovery/)
- [Random Access Memories, Daft Punk](https://www.daftpunk.com/randomaccessmemories/)
- [Justice, Justice](https://etjusticepourtous.bandcamp.com/album/justice)

Cover artwork appears in the app screenshots for release identification and
belongs to its respective rights holders.
